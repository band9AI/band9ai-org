TOEFL iBT · test date

A 0–30 chart scores the old test

The chart in a 2024 forum post expects a section score out of 30. A TOEFL iBT sitting on or after 21 January 2026 does not give you that number to look up.

Direct answer

Before 21 January 2026, each TOEFL iBT section was reported from 0 to 30, and the total was the sum of those four scores. From that date, each section is reported from 1 to 6. A chart built for the 0–30 section score cannot mark a later test. Reading and Listening on the new test are adaptive, and ETS does not publish a correct-answer conversion, so this page will not invent one.

The score report guide explains the comparable 0–120 overall that new reports also print through January 2028. That figure is an estimate of the old total. It is not a section score out of 30, and it is not something you calculate from a blog chart.

Where the old chart still belongs

If the test date is before 21 January 2026, the 0–30 section scores on that report are real. The total is their sum. A chart that explains those scores is about that sitting. It does not reach forward and mark a 2026 test.

ETS also publishes tables that set old 0–30 ranges beside the new 1–6 scores, so institutions can read both eras. Those tables compare reported scores. They are not a key for “I got 28 questions right.” Counting correct answers and sliding a finger down a 0–30 column scores a test the new form does not report that way.

Why there is no new correct-answer table

The updated Reading section is about 50 items. Listening is about 47. Both are adaptive, so the next item depends on earlier answers. A fixed chart of “this many correct equals this 1–6 score” would pretend every candidate sat the same paper. ETS reports the 1–6 section score instead. IELTS Reading does publish average marks out of 40. TOEFL does not give this site that kind of table, and this site will not draw one.

Writing and Speaking on the new test do publish raw point ranges. Those ranges are still not the 1–6 on the report. That is a separate mistake, covered on the Writing and Speaking pages.

Questions

Can I use a 0–30 TOEFL chart on a test taken after 21 January 2026?

From that date the section score on the report is 1 to 6, not 0 to 30. A chart that marks the old section score is the wrong tool for that sitting.

Does the new TOEFL still give section scores out of 30?

Tests on or after 21 January 2026 report each section from 1 to 6. Through January 2028 the report also shows a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale. That overall figure is not a 0–30 section score.

Is there a correct-answer chart for the new Reading or Listening test?

ETS does not publish one. Reading is about 50 items and Listening about 47, and both are adaptive. This page does not invent a conversion from correct answers to a 1–6 score.
