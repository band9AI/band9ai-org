Speaking

Eleven answers. One score from 1 to 6.

Each spoken response can score up to 5. There are eleven of them. Someone who adds those ratings can reach 55. The Speaking box on the score report does not go to 55.

Direct answer

Updated TOEFL iBT Speaking has two task types. Listen and Repeat is seven responses, each scored from 0 to 5. Take an Interview is four responses, each scored from 0 to 5. The raw points can total 55. ETS reports Speaking on the 1–6 scale. That 1–6, not the raw total, is averaged with Reading, Listening, and Writing.

55 is the sum of the maximum ratings. It is not a reported score. The reported Speaking score is 1 to 6.

Check a total before you call it a score

Enter the Listen and Repeat points (0–35) and the interview points (0–20). The note will add them. If the total is above 6, it cannot be the number in the Speaking box.

Those boxes add to 44. A reported Speaking score cannot be 44. The form shows 1 to 6. ETS does not publish a chart that turns 44 raw points into one of those half steps.

The two tasks fail in different ways

Listen and Repeat asks whether the sentence comes back intelligibly: the words, the shape, the stress. Take an Interview asks you to answer in your own words, with enough development that the reply is more than a fragment. Drilling opinions does not repair a repeat item you could not hold. Repeating sentences does not build the interview answer.

IELTS Speaking is four criteria at equal weight, with no raw total of 55. That page is here. A plan written for fluency, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation still has to be re-cut for these two TOEFL tasks. The labels are not the scoring units.

After Speaking is scaled, it is one of four section scores. The raw 44 never enters that average.

 Writing has the same kind of trap

Right-or-wrong sentences, plus two ratings from 0 to 5. The report shows 1 to 6.

 IELTS Speaking has no raw total

 Do not turn 55 into a score out of 120

The comparable 0–120 figure is printed on the report. It is not your raw Speaking points.

Questions

How is TOEFL iBT Speaking scored?

Speaking has 11 responses. Seven are Listen and Repeat, each scored from 0 to 5. Four are Take an Interview, each scored from 0 to 5. The raw points can add to 55. The Speaking score on the report is on the 1–6 scale.

Is a TOEFL Speaking raw total of 40 a score of 4?

Forty is a sum of task points. It is not the 1–6 Speaking score, and it is not four-tenths of 55 turned into a section score. ETS does not publish that conversion on the public scoring page.

Does the interview replace Listen and Repeat?

Both task types are scored. Listen and Repeat is seven items about whether the sentence comes back intelligibly. The interview is four questions about speaking on your own. A strong interview does not erase the repeat items, and the other way around.
