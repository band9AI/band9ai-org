Writing

The points you can add are not the Writing score

TOEFL Writing is three task types. Two of them are paragraphs with a 0–5 rating. Ten of them are sentences that are simply right or wrong. The number on the score report is still a 1–6.

Direct answer

The updated TOEFL iBT Writing section has 12 items. Ten Build a Sentence items score 1 or 0. Write an Email is one response from 0 to 5. Write for an Academic Discussion is one response from 0 to 5. ETS then reports Writing on the 1–6 scale. That 1–6 is the Writing input to the overall average. A homemade sum of the task points is not that score.

Where people train the wrong task

A polished discussion can hide ten sentence items that were wrong. Those ten are not a style score. Each one is right or wrong. The email is a different job from the discussion: a reader, a request, and a register, scored on its own 0–5 rubric. Practising only the discussion leaves the other two tasks untouched.

IELTS Writing is a different test. There, four criteria are averaged inside a task and Task 2 weighs more than Task 1. That mechanic is on the IELTS Writing page. Do not copy an IELTS criterion plan onto these three TOEFL tasks and expect the same arithmetic.

Questions

How is TOEFL iBT Writing scored?

The Writing section has 12 items in three task types. Ten Build a Sentence items are each correct or incorrect. Write an Email is one response scored from 0 to 5. Write for an Academic Discussion is one response scored from 0 to 5. The Writing score on the report is on the 1–6 scale.

If I add my TOEFL Writing points, is that my section score?

A sum of sentence points plus the email and discussion ratings is not the 1–6 Writing score. ETS does not publish a raw-total cut for each half point on the public scoring page. Only the 1–6 Writing score is averaged with the other three sections.

Does the email count more than the academic discussion?

Both written responses are scored from 0 to 5 on their own rubrics. This page does not invent a weight between them. The reported Writing score, after ETS scores the whole section, is one 1–6 number.
