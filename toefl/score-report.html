Score report · through January 2028

The 0–120 number is already on the report

Universities are switching at different speeds. Some still write “100.” Some write “5.” The report for a new test prints both kinds of overall number so you do not have to invent the translation.

Direct answer

Through January 2028, a TOEFL iBT score report for a test on or after 21 January 2026 shows a CEFR level, 1–6 section scores, a 1–6 overall score, and a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale. That 0–120 figure is the midpoint of a range. ETS’s example: a 4.5 is accompanied by 90. Adding the four 1–6 section scores does not produce it.

Two eras of arithmetic

Before 21 January 2026, each section was 0–30 and the total was the sum, from 0 to 120. From that date, each section is 1–6 and the overall score is the average, rounded to the nearest half. The sum rule belongs to the old report. The average rule belongs to the new one. A score from the old test can still be in circulation for about two years, which is why both formats show up in admissions offices through the transition.

On a new report, the extra 0–120 overall is a comparable estimate, not a second way of adding your section scores. ETS says a 4.5 comes with 90 as that estimate, and calls 90 the midpoint of the equivalent range. Use the number on the report. Do not rebuild it from 5 + 4.5 + 5 + 4.

What ETS suggests when a school still says 100

These are ETS’s recommended replacements for common old totals, not a promise that a given university has updated its page. A 5 is the recommendation where the old bar was 100, and ETS also says a 5 corresponds to a range that starts at 95, not to the single score 100. Choosing 5.5 to avoid anyone under 100 will also miss some people whose comparable performance would have cleared 100. Read the school’s current requirement either way.

A TOEFL 5 is not an IELTS 5

ETS publishes a comparison with IELTS. On that table, a TOEFL total of 5 sits beside an IELTS 7, and a TOEFL total of 4.5 sits beside an IELTS 6.5. The note under the table says those totals were rounded up to limit false matches, and that a real overall score can come from different section mixes. It is a concordance, not the calculator on the TOEFL overall page and not the calculator on the IELTS plateau page.

Questions

Why does my new TOEFL report still show a score out of 120?

Through January 2028, ETS prints three things: a CEFR level, the 1–6 section and overall scores, and a comparable overall score on the 0–120 scale. The 0–120 figure is the midpoint of the matching range. ETS’s example: a 4.5 is accompanied by 90.

Can I add my 1–6 section scores to get the old total?

Tests before 21 January 2026 added four section scores of 0–30 into a total of 0–120. On the new scale the overall score is an average from 1 to 6. The comparable 0–120 number is supplied on the report. Adding 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 does not produce 100.

Is a TOEFL 5 the same requirement as a 100?

ETS recommends that an institution which used to require 100 set 5 on the new scale, and it says a 5 lines up with a range that starts at 95. A 5 is not the single old score 100. A school may still publish 100, 5, or both during the transition. Read that school’s page.
