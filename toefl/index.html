TOEFL iBT · from 21 January 2026

The overall score is an average, then a half step

The 1–6 scale replaced the habit of adding four scores out of 30. The new overall score is the average of the four section scores. A low section pulls that average down. It does not replace the other three.

Direct answer

For TOEFL iBT tests on or after 21 January 2026, Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking are each reported from 1 to 6 in half-point steps. The overall score is their average, rounded to the nearest half. ETS’s published examples: an average of 5.125 becomes 5, and an average of 5.25 becomes 5.5. This is not an IELTS band, and it is not a sum out of 120.

The two examples ETS prints

Nearest half means 5.125 is closer to 5 than to 5.5, so it becomes 5. An average of 5.25 sits halfway between 5 and 5.5, and ETS rounds that halfway case up to 5.5. This page uses that same rule for other halfway averages, including .75, because 5.25 is the halfway case they already round up. The rows below are illustrations of that rule. Average four section scores

Use a whole or half score from 1 to 6. The result is the overall score under the published rounding rule. It does not predict a test day, and it does not produce the comparable 0–120 number. That number is explained on the score report page.

Average 5.25 rounds to an overall score of 5.5. This matches ETS’s published example of a 5.25 average.

Reading and Listening do not have an IELTS-style mark chart

The updated Reading section is about 50 items. Listening is about 47. Both are adaptive, so a public “this many correct equals this score” table would be a guess. ETS reports the 1–6 section score. IELTS Listening does publish average marks out of 40. TOEFL does not give this page an equivalent chart, so this page does not invent one.

Writing and Speaking are different. Their tasks have published point ranges, and those ranges are still not the 1–6 score on the report. That split is the Writing page and the Speaking page.

Questions

How is the TOEFL iBT overall score calculated in 2026?

For tests taken on or after 21 January 2026, you receive four section scores and an overall score on a 1–6 scale in half-point steps. The overall score is the average of Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking, rounded to the nearest half. ETS’s examples: an average of 5.125 becomes 5, and an average of 5.25 becomes 5.5.

Does one low TOEFL section become the overall score?

It pulls the average down. Three sections at 5 and one section at 4 average 4.75. Using the same nearest-half rule as ETS’s published 5.25 example, 4.75 rounds up to 5. Three sections at 5 and one at 3.5 average 4.625, which is nearer to 4.5.

Is there a correct-answer chart for TOEFL Reading?

Not in the way IELTS publishes average marks out of 40. The updated TOEFL Reading and Listening sections are adaptive. ETS reports a 1–6 section score. This page does not invent a raw-score conversion.
