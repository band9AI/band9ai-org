The plateau / Writing cap

Writing

The lowest criterion is not your band

A Writing script is not graded by its worst line. Each task gets four scores. Those four are averaged. Task 2 then counts more than Task 1 toward the Writing band.

Direct answer

IELTS Writing uses four criteria on each task: task achievement or task response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range and accuracy. They are weighted equally and averaged. Task 2 carries more weight in the Writing band than Task 1. A single weak criterion pulls that task’s average down. It does not become the Writing band by itself.

The four criteria

Select a criterion to read the kind of miss that keeps it at Band 6. The descriptions are plain-language study notes, not the official descriptor text.

Task Response is the Task 2 name. Task 1 calls the same job Task Achievement: cover what the prompt actually asks. On Task 2, a Band 6 script often states a position and then repeats it. The score moves when each main idea gains an example, a reason, or a consequence. Grammar edits will not fix an idea that was never developed.

Average one task yourself

This averages the four criteria for a single task and rounds to a half band. It is not a full Writing score, because Task 1 and Task 2 are assessed separately and Task 2 weighs more. IELTS states that Task 2 carries more weight and does not print the ratio on the public scoring page, so this page does not guess one.

Average 6.75 rounds to a task band of 7.0. The 6 pulls the average down and still leaves the task at 7.

What generic feedback misses

A rewrite can make a sentence smoother and leave the limiting criterion untouched. If the position is clear but each idea is one sentence, the cap is development, not grammar. If every paragraph is developed and the verbs break down, the cap is grammatical range and accuracy. The useful comment names the criterion, says what to change in the next paragraph, and gives you a way to write again under time.

Questions

Does my lowest Writing criterion become my Writing band?

No. On each task, IELTS averages four equally weighted criteria and rounds. A Task Response of 6 beside coherence, vocabulary, and grammar at 7 averages 6.75, which rounds to 7 for that task. The 6 pulls the average down. It does not replace the other three.

Which part of Writing moves the score more?

IELTS says Task 2 carries more weight than Task 1. The public scoring page does not print the ratio, so this guide does not invent one. A weak Task 2 still moves the Writing band more than the same weakness on Task 1.

What is the difference between Task Achievement and Task Response?

Task Achievement is the criterion for Writing Task 1. Task Response is the criterion for Writing Task 2. The other three criteria, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range and accuracy, apply to both tasks.
