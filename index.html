Band 6 – 6.5

Why the band stays put

Most people in this range do not need another random mock. They need to see which skill is holding the average down, then practice that skill on purpose.

Direct answer

Candidates stay at IELTS Band 6 or 6.5 when one skill keeps pulling the four-skill average down. The overall band is that average, rounded to the nearest half band. Practising a skill that is already strong does not move it. A practice estimate can name the weaker skill. It is not an official IELTS score.

How the overall band is built

On the official IELTS scoring page, the overall band is the average of Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, rounded to the nearest half band. If the average ends in .25, it rounds up to the next half band. If it ends in .75, it rounds up to the next whole band.

IELTS publishes this worked example. Listening 6.5, Reading 6.5, Writing 5.0, Speaking 7.0. The average is 6.25, and the reported band is 6.5. Writing did not “become” the overall score. It stopped the average from reaching 7.

Four skills. Usually one of them is the drag.

Listening and Reading are raw marks out of 40, converted to a band. The conversion shifts slightly by test version. IELTS publishes average marks, not a fixed cut score: about 23 for Band 6 and about 30 for Band 7 in Listening and in Academic Reading. Writing and Speaking are criterion scores averaged by an examiner.

Listening is heard once. A run of missed details lowers the raw score, and the band follows the conversion for that paper. More vocabulary study does not repair a listening miss.

Why another month of the same practice fails

The level is still a guess

Untimed work feels fine. The exam is timed, and the band is an average. A guess about “about a 7” is not a section score.

The hours go to the wrong skill

Essays will not lift a Listening paper that is stuck near 23 out of 40. Reading speed will not fix an under-developed Task 2.

The same miss comes back

Without a named limiter, the next mock repeats the last one. The retake fee is the expensive version of that loop.

Questions

Why do candidates stay at Band 6 or 6.5?

The overall band is the average of four skills, rounded to the nearest half band. Extra practice on a skill that is already strong does not lift that average. The skill sitting under the others has to move.

Does my lowest skill become my overall band?

No. IELTS averages Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, then rounds. A 5.0 in Writing beside 6.5, 6.5, and 7.0 averages 6.25, which rounds to 6.5. The low skill pulls the average down. It does not replace the other three scores.

Is a Band9AI result an official IELTS score?

No. Band9AI reports a practice estimate from timed mocks. Official bands are awarded only through an IELTS test. The two numbers can differ.
