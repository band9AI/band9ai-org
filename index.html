Score guides

Two tests. Two scoring rules.

IELTS reports bands from 0 to 9. TOEFL iBT, for tests from 21 January 2026, reports 1 to 6. Both overall scores are averages. The scales, the sections, and the rounding examples are not the same number.

Direct answer

Use the IELTS guides when the score is a band on a Test Report Form. Use the TOEFL guides when the score is a TOEFL iBT result on the 1–6 scale. Band9AI’s timed practice and the $15 Reality Check are IELTS estimates on band9ai.com. They are not a TOEFL score, and neither test’s practice result replaces the official one.

IELTS

The overall band averages Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, then rounds to the nearest half band. A .25 average rounds up. Listening and Reading start as marks out of 40.

TOEFL iBT

From 21 January 2026 the overall score averages four section scores on a 1–6 scale and rounds to the nearest half. A reported 0–120 figure on a new score report is a comparable estimate, not a sum of the 1–6 scores.

What not to mix

A TOEFL 5 is not an IELTS 5. ETS publishes its own comparison, and on that table an IELTS 7 sits beside a TOEFL total of 5, while an IELTS 6.5 sits beside a TOEFL total of 4.5. That table is a concordance for institutions. It is not the rounding rule a test taker uses on exam day. The IELTS rule is on the IELTS plateau page. The TOEFL rule, including the published examples 5.125 rounds to 5 and 5.25 rounds to 5.5, is on the TOEFL overall page.

Which test you should sit is the list on the university or visa page, not a preference for one format. Band9AI’s note on that distinction is IELTS vs TOEFL for immigration.

Questions

Does Band9AI score TOEFL?

No. The free estimate and the $15 Reality Check on band9ai.com are IELTS practice. A result there is not a TOEFL score and not an official IELTS Test Report Form.

Is a TOEFL 5 the same as an IELTS 5?

No. They are different scales. ETS’s comparison puts a TOEFL total of 5 beside an IELTS 7, not beside an IELTS 5. Use each test’s own scoring page before you treat one number as the other.
