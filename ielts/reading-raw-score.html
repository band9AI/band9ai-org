Reading

The same 30 correct is not the same band

Academic and General Training Reading both have 40 questions and the same nine-band scale. The average raw score behind a band is higher on General Training. A chart from the other module will send you to the wrong study target.

Direct answer

On Academic Reading, IELTS publishes average marks of 15 for Band 5, 23 for Band 6, 30 for Band 7, and 35 for Band 8. On General Training Reading, the averages are 15 for Band 4, 23 for Band 5, 30 for Band 6, and 35 for Band 7. Thirty correct answers sits at the Band 7 average on Academic and at the Band 6 average on General Training. The precise cut shifts slightly by test version.

The section band is what the overall average uses

Whichever module you sit, the Reading band on the form is averaged with Listening, Writing, and Speaking, then rounded to the nearest half band. The Academic row makes a General Training raw score look higher than the General Training average. Twenty-three correct is the Academic Band 6 average and the General Training Band 5 average. Thirty correct is Academic Band 7 and General Training Band 6. Using the other module’s row points the study target at the wrong band.

The section band, once you have the right row, is still only one input. General Training Reading at its Band 6 average, beside Listening 7.0, Writing 7.0, and Speaking 7.0, averages 6.75 and rounds to an overall 7.0. The Reading paper can sit on that Band 6 average and the headline band can still be 7.0, because the other three carry it.

Unfinished passages are the usual Reading stall. The clock runs out with answers still blank, and a blank scores the same as a wrong answer: zero. The raw score moves when more questions are reached and the traps (a word copied from the wrong sentence, a True that is only half supported) stop costing marks. A longer vocabulary list does not time the passage for you.

Questions

How many correct answers for IELTS Academic Reading Band 7?

IELTS publishes 30 out of 40 as the average number of Academic Reading marks at Band 7. Band 6 averages 23, Band 5 averages 15, and Band 8 averages 35. The precise number shifts slightly by test version.

Why does General Training Reading need more correct answers?

Both modules use the same nine-band scale. Academic texts can be harder in vocabulary and style, so IELTS says it is usual that General Training needs more correct answers for the same band. The published General Training averages are 15 for Band 4, 23 for Band 5, 30 for Band 6, and 35 for Band 7.

Is 23 out of 40 a Band 6 on both Reading tests?

23 is the published average for Band 6 on Academic Reading and for Band 5 on General Training Reading. Using the Academic row on a General Training paper makes those marks look one band higher than the General Training average.
