Listening

Band 7 Listening is about 30 out of 40

The raw score is a count of correct answers. The section band is what enters the overall average. Those are two different numbers, and only one of them appears on the Test Report Form beside the word Listening.

Direct answer

IELTS Listening has 40 questions, one mark each. The published averages are 16 for Band 5, 23 for Band 6, 30 for Band 7, and 35 for Band 8. The exact cut shifts slightly by test version. A Listening band is one of four equal inputs to the overall score. Moving it from 6.0 to 7.0 changes the average by 0.25, which is not always enough to change the reported overall band.

The averages IELTS actually prints

Each correct answer scores 1. Scores out of 40 convert to the nine-band scale and are reported in whole and half bands. IELTS prints the average marks at four bands and says the precise number needed varies slightly from test version to test version. This page stops where that table stops. It does not fill in Band 6.5 or Band 9 with a made-up cut score.

27 is above the published Band 6 average of 23 and below the Band 7 average of 30. That is not a Band 6.5. IELTS does not print a raw mark for 6.5, and the cut on a given paper can shift.

When the section moves and the overall does not

The overall band is the average of Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, rounded to the nearest half band. An average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band. An average ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band. One skill moving by 1.0 changes that average by 0.25.

Hold the other three skills at 6.5. Listening at the Band 6 average and Listening at the Band 7 average produce the same reported overall: 6.5. The overall reaches 7.0 in this pattern when Listening itself is 7.5. Seven extra correct answers, the distance from 23 to 30, can be real progress on the Listening paper and still leave the headline band where it was.

What actually drops Listening marks

The recording is played once. A run of missed details is a raw-score problem: the answer was in the audio and it was not written down. Vocabulary study after the fact does not restore those marks. The useful split is whether the miss was the word itself, a distractor later in the sentence, or spelling and word limit on the answer sheet. Those are different drills.

Questions

How many correct answers for IELTS Listening Band 7?

IELTS publishes 30 out of 40 as the average number of Listening marks at Band 7. Band 6 averages 23, Band 5 averages 16, and Band 8 averages 35. The precise number shifts slightly from test version to test version.

Is 30 out of 40 always Listening Band 7?

30 is the published average at Band 7, not a fixed cut score. IELTS says the precise number of marks needed varies slightly by test version. This page does not invent a mark for every half band.

If I raise Listening from Band 6 to Band 7, does my overall band rise?

Sometimes. One section band changes the four-skill average by 0.25. If Reading, Writing, and Speaking are each 6.5, Listening 6.0 averages 6.375 and rounds to 6.5, and Listening 7.0 averages 6.625 and still rounds to 6.5. In that pattern the overall reaches 7.0 when Listening is 7.5.
