Writing

Task 2 is not half the Writing band

Add the two task scores and divide by two, and both of the scripts below become 6.5. They are not the same Writing result. Task 2 counts more, and IELTS does not say how much.

Direct answer

The IELTS Writing band is not a 50/50 average of Task 1 and Task 2. Each task is assessed on its own: four criteria, equal weight, averaged inside that task. Task 2 then carries more weight in the Writing band than Task 1. The public scoring page states that order and does not print the ratio, so this guide will not invent one.

See the equal average, then stop

Enter a whole or half band for each task. The note shows the 50/50 figure and refuses to turn it into a Writing band.

An equal average of 7.0 and 6.0 is 6.5. That number treats both tasks the same. Task 2 weighs more, and the ratio is not published, so 6.5 is not a Writing band.

What you can say without the ratio

When Task 1 and Task 2 are the same band, extra weight on Task 2 cannot pull the combination away from that band. When they differ, the lower score hurts more if it sits on Task 2 than if it sits on Task 1. That is the whole public rule. A website that prints “Task 2 is 67 percent” is guessing. This one will not.

Train the task that can move the Writing band further. That is Task 2: the position, the development, and the time you actually spend on it. A polished Task 1 does not cancel a Task 2 that never left the prompt. The official Writing band still comes from an IELTS examiner.

Questions

Is the IELTS Writing band the average of Task 1 and Task 2?

Each task is assessed on its own. Task 2 then carries more weight than Task 1. An equal average of the two task scores is not the Writing band.

What weight does IELTS give Task 2?

IELTS says Task 2 carries more weight than Task 1. The public scoring page does not print the ratio, so this guide does not invent one.

Can I calculate my Writing band from both task scores?

When the two task scores differ, the lower Task 2 pulls harder than the same low score on Task 1. Without the published ratio you cannot turn those two numbers into the official Writing band.
